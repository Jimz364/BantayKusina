Storage Guide

Simple reminders for storing common household food.

🥩 Meat and poultry

Keep raw meat sealed on the lowest refrigerator shelf to prevent drips. Freeze portions you will not use soon. Thaw in the refrigerator, not on the counter.

Keep raw and ready-to-eat food separate.

🐟 Seafood

Refrigerate seafood promptly in a covered container. Use fresh seafood soon or freeze it if you need to keep it longer.

Keep it cold and avoid cross-contamination.

🥬 Leafy vegetables

Store leafy greens in the refrigerator. Keep them dry and use a breathable bag or container. Wash close to the time you plan to use them.

Remove damaged leaves and check often.

🍌 Fruits

Some fruits ripen faster at room temperature. Refrigerate ripe fruit when appropriate, and keep ethylene-producing fruits away from sensitive produce.

Check ripeness before planning meals.

🥚 Eggs and dairy

Keep eggs and dairy refrigerated. Store milk and other perishables toward the back of the refrigerator, where temperatures stay more stable.

Follow package dates and local food-safety advice.

🍚 Rice and leftovers

Cool cooked food promptly in shallow containers and refrigerate. Reheat leftovers thoroughly. Avoid leaving cooked rice or meals at room temperature for long periods.

Label containers with the date they were prepared.

🥖 Bread and dry goods

Keep bread sealed in a cool, dry place, or freeze portions for longer storage. Store rice, flour, and other dry goods in clean airtight containers.

Protect dry goods from moisture and pests.

🧊 Freezer basics

Use freezer-safe packaging, remove excess air, and label food with its name and date. Freeze in meal-sized portions to make future use easier.

Use safe thawing methods before cooking.
This guide offers general storage reminders, not a complete food-safety reference. Follow product labels and guidance from trusted food-safety authorities.
